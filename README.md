# Auto-Loan Research Exercise: Literature Review and Proposal

- [Literature review](lit_review.pdf), revised 6 November 2026: findings from the literature on household vehicle-purchase timing, the evidence and limits of the selected models, and implications for the JPMorgan research question. The 11-page main text is followed by technical appendices containing equations, derivations, source checks, and the reading agenda. The full document has 52 pages, including references.
- [Utility specifications and decision period](utility_ladder.pdf): eight utility specifications, from a static choice with at most one car to a dynamic model with latent need. Each states the state, the menu, the utility, the forecast, what it adds, and what it cannot do. A second section discusses the choice of decision period given that purchases are rare. No parameters are estimated, and identification is not yet shown for any version.
- [Research proposal](proposal.pdf), 23 October 2026: utility, research questions, provisional formulation, and the sources of the model components. References to the revised literature review were refreshed on 6 November 2026.

These are discussion drafts for Eddie. The literature review separates established results, empirical findings, and unresolved requirements for this application. The proposal has not been empirically validated.
